Trip journal

Day by day, preserving the little details that make the trip memorable.

Aug 6

Tel Aviv → Budapest → Veszprém

  • EL AL flight to Budapest; dinner at McDonald’s; roughly two-hour drive to Veszprém.
  • Hotel Historia Malomkert; arrived around 1:30 a.m.
Airport legend: Matan’s bag caused the police to clear the coffee-shop seating area before making him open it — at age nine.
Aug 7

BalatoniBob & Tihany

  • Mountain bobsledding; lunch at Susogo Etterem.
  • Back to BalatoniBob for zipline, archery and ice cream.
  • Walked Tihany’s main street and drove the scenic peninsula route past swans.
  • Dinner at Giorgi Pizzeria.
Aug 8

Birthday on Lake Balaton

  • Midnight cheesecake, candles and balloons.
  • Mall morning; afternoon tree trekking at Kalandziget Tihany.
  • Swam in Lake Balaton with inflatables; dinner in Balatonfüred.
  • Moved to Hotel Kalma in Hévíz.
Aug 9

Hévíz

  • Breakfast at Zalaco bakery.
  • Thermal Lake Hévíz.
  • McDonald’s lunch; moved to Kék Vendégház.
Aug 10

Zalakaros water day

  • Zalakaros water park.
  • Dinner at a cute Italian restaurant in Zalakaros.
Aug 11

Keszthely, Tapolca & ferry to Siófok

  • Walked the Keszthely boardwalk; visited Tapolca caves.
  • Stopped for palacsinta; drove the car onto the Tihany–Zamárdi ferry.
  • Dinner at Johnny’s Diner; checked into Platan Hotel.
Aug 12

Siófok beach day

  • Breakfast in a cute café in town.
  • Beach day in Siófok.
  • American-style 50s diner for dinner — memorable for the wrong reasons.
Aug 13

Paddle boat day

  • Breakfast at Cafe Matan on the boardwalk — excellent eggs Benedict.
  • Rented the “police” paddle boat with the slide.
  • Dinner at Mike’s Restaurant; Talya got a hair braid.
Aug 14

Zamárdi → Budapest

  • Zamárdi Adventure Park: sifting for gold.
  • Go-karting.
  • Drove to Budapest; KFC on the way; checked into 7Seasons Apartments.
Aug 15

Central Budapest

  • IKONO sensory museum.
  • Váci utca pedestrian mall and Central Market Hall.
  • Westend Mall; met Agi Gonda.
Aug 16

Aquaworld

  • Aquapark water-park day.
  • Takeaway dinner: chicken paprikash, pasta, and Jamie Oliver fish & chips.
Aug 17

Synagogue & Chain Bridge

  • Dohány Street Synagogue.
  • Széchenyi Chain Bridge below the castle.
  • Met Aniko Khul at Dunapark Kávéház; Westend Mall.
Aug 18

Last Budapest day & flight home

  • Gozsdu Court market stalls; indoor glow-in-the-dark mini-golf.
  • Premier Outlets; airport.
  • Late-night EL AL flight home.
